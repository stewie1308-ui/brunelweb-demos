Bonding before veneers

Composite bonding is additive: resin is shaped onto the tooth in one visit and no healthy enamel is removed. If you dislike it, it comes off and you are back where you started.

Veneers are irreversible. Preparing a tooth for one means removing enamel permanently, and that tooth will need a veneer or crown for the rest of your life. Sometimes that is the right call — for heavily discoloured or misshapen teeth it may be the only one that works — but it should never be the first thing suggested.

  • Bonding: one visit, no drilling, reversible, lasts 5 to 8 years
  • Veneers: two visits, enamel removed permanently, lasts 10 to 15 years
  • Bonding stains over time and can be polished or replaced

Whitening, and the law

In the UK, tooth whitening is a dental procedure and may only be carried out by registered dental professionals. Products sold to the public are legally capped at 0.1% hydrogen peroxide; a dentist may use up to 6%.

That is the difference between the kits online and what actually works. It is also why beauty salons offering whitening are acting illegally, whatever they tell you.

Whitening does not change crowns, veneers or existing fillings. If you have visible restorations, they will need replacing afterwards to match, and we will tell you that before you start rather than after.

What we will not do

We do not prepare healthy front teeth for veneers on request alone. If the shape or colour can be dealt with by bonding, orthodontics or whitening, that is what we will offer.

It is the single most common regret in cosmetic dentistry and it cannot be undone.

Questions people ask about cosmetic work

How long does bonding last?

Five to eight years is typical before it needs polishing or replacing. It stains more readily than enamel, so red wine, coffee and smoking shorten that. It is a maintenance treatment rather than a permanent one, and the price reflects that.

Can I have a "Turkey teeth" style result here?

No. What is usually meant by that is crowns on heavily reduced healthy teeth, and we will not do it. We can discuss what is achievable with bonding, whitening and alignment, which for most people gets closer to what they actually wanted anyway.

Will whitening make my teeth sensitive?

Commonly, yes, for a few days. It is temporary and it settles. Sensitive toothpaste for a fortnight beforehand reduces it, and we can slow the schedule down if it becomes uncomfortable.

Bring a photograph of what bothers you.

It is easier to talk about a specific tooth than a general feeling, and it makes the consultation far more useful.
