Common questions

The things people ring up to ask.

I have not been to a dentist in years.

That is most of our new patients, and it is not a conversation you need to brace for. The first appointment is an assessment, not a lecture, and nothing gets done to you on the day unless you ask for it.

How do people usually pay for this?

Routine work is paid on the day. Larger treatment plans are usually spread over twelve months at 0% through our finance provider, subject to status. We will show you the monthly figure alongside the total before you decide.

Do you take NHS patients?

No, we are a wholly private practice. We are straightforward about that up front because it is the first thing worth knowing, and we will tell you if what you need would be better done on the NHS elsewhere.

What if I am in pain today?

Phone before 9am and we will find you a slot the same day. The assessment is £95 and comes off the cost of whatever treatment follows.
