before you commit.

A digital scan at the consultation produces a projection of where your teeth would end up. If you do not like it, you have lost an hour rather than three thousand pounds.

The part people underestimate

Twenty-two hours a day. Aligners only move teeth while they are in, and the most common reason treatment overruns is that they were not worn enough. They come out to eat and to clean your teeth, and that is it.

If that sounds unrealistic for your life, say so at the consultation. Fixed braces are less convenient but they do not depend on you.

Attachments, and what it looks like

Most cases need small tooth-coloured bumps bonded to some teeth so the aligners have something to grip. They are removed at the end and they are the reason "invisible" is an overstatement — discreet is honest, invisible is not.

Some cases also need a small amount of enamel polished between teeth to create space. It is measured in fractions of a millimetre and it is discussed before it happens, not during.

Retainers are permanent

Teeth move back. Not sometimes — always, if nothing holds them. Everyone finishes with retainers and everyone keeps wearing them, most commonly at night for good.

Your first set is included. Replacements are £180 a set, and people do lose them.

Questions people ask about aligners

Will it fix my bite or just the look?

Depends on the bite. Aligners handle crowding, spacing and mild to moderate bite problems well. Significant skeletal discrepancies are an orthodontic or surgical question, and if that is what you have we will say so and refer you rather than take on something aligners cannot fix.

Does it hurt?

Each new aligner feels tight for a day or two, which is the point — that pressure is what moves the teeth. It is pressure rather than pain, and most people change aligners at night so the worst of it happens while asleep.

Can I get it cheaper online?

Yes, and we would rather you understood the difference. Remote aligner services work from an impression you take yourself with no clinical examination, no X-rays and nobody checking your gums. Moving teeth with undiagnosed gum disease can lose them. That is the risk you are pricing.
