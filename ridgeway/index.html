The work
The stone

Bath and Cotswold,
matched to the bed

We buy block from the same two quarries every time, so the colour and the grain match what is already standing on your wall. Wrong bed, wrong stone, and it weathers apart inside ten years.

The finish

Tooled by hand,
never polished.

The cut

Marked, banked
and worked by hand

Every face is set out full size, cut on the banker and finished with a chisel rather than a saw. You can read the tooling from across the room, and that is the point.

Cut once, and still standing in three hundred years.

New work

Fireplaces and openings

Surrounds, hearths, mullions, heads, cills and porticos. Drawn with you, cut to the millimetre, fitted by the people who cut it.

Restoration

Matched to what is there

Indent repairs, lime re-pointing, and replacement stone cut to the original profile. Listed and conservation work welcome.
