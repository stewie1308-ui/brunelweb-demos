Indent repairs, lime repointing and replacement stone matched to what is already standing. Listed buildings and conservation areas welcome.

Matching the stone

Getting the colour right is the easy half. The harder half is the bed: stone comes out of the ground in layers, and it has to be set the same way up as it formed, or the face spalls off in the first hard frost.

We buy from the same two quarries every time and keep offcuts from each, so a repair made in ten years still matches the one made today.

Indent repairs

Where a face has decayed but the wall behind it is sound, we cut the damaged stone back to a square pocket and let in a new piece, rather than replacing the whole block.

Less stone removed, less disturbance to the wall, and considerably cheaper than full replacement.

  • Cut back to sound stone, never feathered at the edges
  • Bedded in lime, never cement
  • Tooled to match the surrounding surface

Pointing, and why lime

Old walls are built to let water out through the joints. Cement pointing seals them, so the water leaves through the stone instead, and takes the face with it.

We repoint in lime mortar, deliberately softer than the stone around it, so the joint stays the sacrificial part. It cures slowly and cannot be done in a frost, which is why this work is booked between spring and autumn.

Listed buildings

Listed building consent is the owner’s responsibility rather than ours, but we will provide the specification and the sample panels a conservation officer will ask for, and we are used to working to one.
