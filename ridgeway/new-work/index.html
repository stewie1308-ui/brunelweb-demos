Fireplaces, window surrounds, porticos and garden stonework, set out full size and worked by hand in the Chippenham workshop.

Fireplaces and hearths

Most of what we make. A surround is drawn to the opening you actually have, not chosen from a page, because a chimney breast is rarely as square as a catalogue assumes.

We cut the jambs, lintel and shelf from the same block wherever the size allows, so the colour runs continuously across the piece instead of changing at every joint.

  • Bolection, Tudor arch, plain bolster and moulded profiles
  • Hearths in one piece up to about 1.8 metres
  • Fitted by the people who cut it

Windows and openings

Mullions, transoms, heads, cills and label moulds. On a new opening we work from your drawings. On an existing one we template the hole first, because the wall has usually moved since it was built.

  • Mullioned and transomed windows
  • Door surrounds and porticos
  • Quoins, kneelers and copings

How long it takes

A fireplace surround is usually six to eight weeks from agreeing the drawing, and most of that is waiting on block from the quarry rather than cutting.

Larger jobs are quoted with a date. If the quarry lets us down you hear about it the week it happens, not the week before fitting.

Send us the opening and we will draw something to it.

A photograph and three measurements is enough to start. There is no charge for the drawing.
