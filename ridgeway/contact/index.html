Bring us the drawing,
or just the problem.

Most enquiries start with a photograph and a rough measurement. That is usually enough for a price, and there is no charge for a drawing.

What helps us quote

The more of this you have, the closer the first number will be. None of it is essential.

  • A photograph of the opening, the wall or the piece
  • Rough measurements, even paced out
  • Whether the building is listed or in a conservation area
  • Roughly when you would like it done

How we work

Enquiries are answered within a working day. Quotes are itemised, in writing, and hold for ninety days.

For anything over a few thousand pounds we take a deposit against the stone, because the block is ordered specifically for the job and cannot go back.
